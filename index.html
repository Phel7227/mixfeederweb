Calorie scaler

Use Calorie scaler to balance calories each day when you feed a mix of kibble and raw diet. It works out your dog's daily calorie target, then splits it between kibble and raw so the total stays in balance, with a raw recipe in grams.

Enter your dog's details and feeding plan to see a daily calorie target, the kibble and raw split, and a raw recipe in grams.

This calculator is a guide only. The results are estimates to help you plan your dog's feeding. Consult your vet before changing your dog's diet.

Your dog

Not sure which shape? Compare body condition scores

Body condition score runs from 1 (very thin) to 5 (heavy). A score of 3 is ideal. Run your hand along the ribs with light pressure, then look at your dog from above and from the side.

Body condition score, how the ribs feel, and the body shape option it matches
ScoreHow the ribs feel when you touch themOption to choose
1 Ribs can be felt with no fat cover. With light pressure they feel sharp, and you can count each one easily. Skinny (underweight)
2 Ribs are easy to feel with only a very thin layer of fat. They feel close to the surface. Skinny (underweight)
3 Ribs can be felt with light pressure under a thin, even layer of fat. They should not be visible. Ideal
4 Ribs are harder to feel and need firmer pressure under a layer of fat. Fat (overweight)
5 Ribs are very hard to feel under a thick layer of fat. The waist is not visible. Fat (overweight)
